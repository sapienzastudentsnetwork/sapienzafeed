17/09/2026 - Cybersecurity has organised an orientation meeting for students.

Students are hereby informed that the Chair of the MSc Programme in Cybersecurity has organised an orientation meeting for students.
The meeting will be held on 25 September 2026 at 11:30 a.m. in Aula I Caglioti (CU032-E01PTEL001).

During the meeting, Prof. Venturi, Prof. Bonomi, and Prof. Lazzeretti will provide information and guidance on a range of topics, including study plans, Master’s theses, part-time enrollment, the Erasmus Programme, and internship opportunities with companies.

16/09/2026 - a.y. 25-26 NOTICE – II EXTRAORDINARY CYBERSECURITY EXAM SESSION

The II Extraordinary Cybersecurity Exam Session will take place from Wednesday, October 7, 2026 to Thursday, November 5, 2026.

In order to participate in the session, students must register through the dedicated form
https://forms.gle/i62oFtVmhd8QgG2f9
no later than Thursday, September 24, 2026 at 2:00 PM.

Registration is necessary to organize the session and determine the examination room for each lecturer.
Students who fall within the categories specified by the Regulations may participate, including students who were out of course in the 2025/2026 academic year, graduating students, and working students, in accordance with the Regulations.

Please note: registration through the form does not replace the official exam booking on Infostud. Once the lecturer opens the relevant exam call, students must book the exam on Infostud, as for any other examination.

Form registration deadline: Thursday, September 24, 2026 at 2:00 PM.
