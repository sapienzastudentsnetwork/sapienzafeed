Industrial Liaison Program
In the M.Sc. Degree in Data Science ↗, we connect students with societally-relevant areas of impact thanks to companies and research centers that actively participate to our Industrial Liaison Program.
We encourage our students to participate in internships in Italy and abroad, as part of their educational curriculum, as additional activities, or during their final project. The Industrial Liaison Program encourages this by organizing the ILP Day, a yearly event for connecting students with companies, public administrations, and industrial and public research institutes.
Are you a company interested in joining the ILP or a student in doubt? Please contact the current ILP supervisor, Prof. Simone Scardapane ↗, for all queries.

The ILP day
Our next ILP day is tentatively scheduled for February, 2026. If you are a student, you can browse the material from last year on the shared Google Drive folders in order to learn more about existing opportunities. More details will be published on this webpage soon.
