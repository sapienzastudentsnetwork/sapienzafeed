25/08/2026 - Complementary Educational Activities

Academic Year 2026/2027
Students may obtain the 6 ECTS credits required for a Complementary Educational Activity (AFC) through one of the following options:

  1. Choose an AFC offered by the Degree Programme
    Select one of the AFCs offered each year by the Degree Programme, which include both classroom teaching and individual work.
  2. Carry out a structured activity proposed by a lecturer
    Undertake a structured activity proposed by a lecturer, involving individual work but no classroom teaching.
  3. Agree on an individual project with a lecturer
    Agree with a lecturer on a topic for individual work, related either to a course taught by the lecturer or to their research activities.
  4. Take an examination in an additional course
    Pass an examination for a course not included in the student's study plan, which may subsequently be officially recorded as an AFC.
  5. Obtain recognition of an extracurricular activity
    Obtain recognition from the Committee for the Recognition of Extracurricular Activities for an activity carried out outside the University, to be validated as an AFC.
