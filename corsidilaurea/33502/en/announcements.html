24/11/2026 - Excellence Programmes (Academic Year 2026–2027)

Portale Trasparenza Amministrazione trasparente - BANDO N. 11/2026 - MODALITA' DI AMMISSIONE AI PERCORSI DI ECCELLENZA - FACOLTÀ I3S - A.A. 2026/2027 ↗

Applications are now open for the

Excellence Programmes (Percorsi di Eccellenza)

for all Bachelor's and Master's Degree Programmes offered by the Faculty of Information Engineering, Computer Science and Statistics at Sapienza University of Rome.

Important Deadlines

Application submission – by 22 January 2027

Applicants must:

  • Complete

    Annex A

    , available in the official call for applications.

  • Attach a transcript of examinations taken, downloaded from Infostud (no revenue stamp required).

  • Submit the documentation by email or in person to the relevant office of their Degree Programme no later than

    22 January 2027

    .

Publication of rankings – by 12 February 2027

The merit-based rankings will be published by

12 February 2027

on:

  • The University's Transparency Administration Portal.

  • The University's Degree Programmes platform.

  • The Faculty's website.

Eligibility Requirements and Application Procedures

Detailed eligibility requirements are specified for each Degree Programme in the official call.

As a general rule, applicants must:

  • Have earned all credits (ECTS) required for the first year by

    30 November 2026

    .

  • Have a weighted grade average of at least

    27/30

    .

Different requirements may apply to certain Master's Degree Programmes.

The number of available places varies depending on the Degree Programme and is specified in the official call.

Final Recognition and Award

Upon successful completion of the Excellence Programme, students will receive official certification of the activities carried out.

In addition, the University will grant an award equivalent to the tuition fees paid during the final year of the Degree Programme, excluding regional taxes and the fixed university contribution.

10/10/2026 - Notice – Extraordinary Examination Session

Notice – Extraordinary Examination Session
Students who have duly registered for the examination through Infostud will be allowed to take the exam even if they have not completed the form or submitted the required notification by 8:00 a.m. on October 9.
On the day of the examination, students may be asked to provide documentation confirming their eligibility to participate in the extraordinary examination session.
Students who decide not to take the exam are also kindly requested to communicate their withdrawal as soon as possible to facilitate the organization of the examination.

07/10/2026 - Erasmus and Free Mover Examination Certificates – DiSCo Lazio

Please note that Erasmus and Free Mover students, as well as students participating in activities within the framework of DiSCo Lazio, cannot be issued certificates or statements confirming the completion of individual exam modules that have not been officially recorded.
Only examinations that have been officially recorded in the student’s academic record, in accordance with University procedures, will be considered valid and may be certified.
Students requiring official documentation concerning completed examinations should first consult their academic record on Infostud and, if necessary, contact the Faculty Student Office: https://www.uniroma1.it/it/pagina/segreteria-amministrativa-studenti-di-ingegneria-dellinformazione-informatica-e-statistica ↗

02/10/2026 - Study Plan

Study Plan Submission
Students are informed that the study plan submission windows are now open.
Students who experience any issues while completing their study plan are invited to consult the information provided in the “Attending – How to complete the study plan” section and, if necessary, contact the relevant contact persons listed in that section.
In case of inability to access the study plan submission procedure, please send a report exclusively from your official Sapienza student email account, to segr.didattica@di.uniroma1.it ↗specifying:

  • Full name of the degree programme
  • Academic year in which you enrolled in the first year
